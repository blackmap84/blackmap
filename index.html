02 — MARKETING AUTOMATIONCORE SERVICE / 01

One reference.
An entire ad workflow.

Growth hacking through an autonomous advertising
pipeline orchestrated by Claude.

Starting with a single reference link from the Meta Ad Library, our pipeline connects creative analysis, product selection, production, publishing, and performance evaluation.

  1. 01 ↗

    ANALYZE

    Understand the creative

    Claude breaks down the reference ad’s cuts, captions, voiceover, and music to understand its structure.

  2. 02 ↗

    PLAN

    Find the right angle

    It selects a relevant client product and marketing angle, then writes a cut-by-cut script while filtering out unsupported claims.

  3. 03 ↗

    PRODUCE

    Build the final ad

    ElevenLabs generates voiceovers. Higgsfield generates video clips, including lip-synced talking shots. The pipeline edits the short-form ad and checks it against the reference.

  4. 04 ↗

    PUBLISH & LEARN

    Measure what happens

    Claude uploads creatives through the Meta Marketing API and automatically evaluates each ad’s performance.

UNIT ECONOMICS, BY DESIGN

Every run logs processing time, token usage, and generation costs, so we can measure production economics and improve the workflow.
